Questions, answered

How much does sump cleaning cost?

Underground sump cleaning starts at Rs.1,999 for tanks up to 5,000 litres. Overhead tanks start at Rs.1,499. Call for an exact quote after free inspection.

How long does it take?

An overhead tank takes 60-90 minutes. An underground sump of 5,000 litres takes 2-3 hours.

Do I need to empty the tank first?

No. The crew drains the tank mechanically. You only provide tank access, a power point and water.

Are the chemicals safe for drinking water?

Yes. Food-grade disinfectants compliant with IS 10500, rinsed and residue-checked before refill.
